# About Us

This website was written and built by **Ruben Geert van den Berg** from Amsterdam, the Netherlands.

![Ruben Geert van den Berg](2025/06/21/000%20SPSS/00%20SPSS入门教程/about-us/medias/49d0ed711a5a768074d0.png)

I studied at the University of Amsterdam where I graduated with distinction in Psychological Research Methods. After that, I started working for **[![](2025/06/21/000%20SPSS/00%20SPSS入门教程/_files_/img-tns-nipo-logo.png.png)TNS NIPO](http://www.tns-nipo.com)** where I further specialized in Data Analysis and SPSS. Working on a huge range of different projects here, I started writing my own tools for speeding up common tasks.

Since several years, I've been a very active member of several SPSS User Communities. Here I noticed that many questions were asked -and answered- over and over again. I therefore wanted to write some tutorials targeting the most common FAQ's, which is how this website started.

If you appreciate my work, feel free to connect with me on **[![](2025/06/21/000%20SPSS/00%20SPSS入门教程/_files_/img-linkedin-logo.png.png)LinkedIn](http://www.linkedin.com/pub/ruben-geert-van-den-berg/a/625/760/)**. If you do so, please do leave me a brief note on how you got in touch with me and in which way -if any- I could assist you.
